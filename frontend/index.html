    How to play

    • You are shown a famous starting XI with the names hidden.
    • Type any player you recognise — order does not matter, and the pitch fills in as you go.
    • Surnames are enough (Beckham), accents are optional (Pique works for Piqué) and small typos are forgiven.
    • If two players in the same XI share a surname, you will be asked for a first name.
    • Initials shows the initials of everyone still hidden. Give me one hands over a player. Both cost points.
    • Naming all eleven before the whistle earns a completion bonus plus whatever time is left.
