          How to play

          • Twelve questions, ten minutes. One clock for the whole round, not one per question. Move between questions freely — skip the hard ones and come back.
          • 100 points a question. Answers are matched generously: near enough spelling counts, and accents and punctuation are optional.
          • Clues cost points. The number of letters is 10, the first letter 20, an anagram 65, four options to choose from 75. Buy what you need and no more.
          • Giving up on one is free. Revealing an answer costs nothing, but that question scores nothing — and it ends any hope of the bonuses.
          • The bonuses need a clean sheet. All twelve right earns 250, plus 3 a second for whatever is left on the clock. Do it without buying a single clue and that is another 250.
          • One go at each pack. Your first attempt is the one the leaderboard keeps, so no grinding it out.
